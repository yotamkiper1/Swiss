# Changes

## 1.0

- Downloads in Recent: turn on **Downloads** at the top of Recent. New downloads land there, and so does anything saved to **Downloads ▸ Swiss**. Finder: right-click ▸ Services ▸ **Add to Swiss Recent**. Chrome: the right-click extension in `extras/chrome`.
- Share from a right-click (AirDrop, Mail, Messages, WhatsApp and more).
- The "you copied…" banner disappears after 15 seconds or when you move to another project or folder.
- A welcome tour on first launch (Help ▸ Welcome Tour shows it again).
- Copy and paste cards and folders inside Swiss, with numbered names.
- The Frame: resize from any corner or side, notes and quotes that scale with their card, pasted text as a quote, a link's web page live on the canvas (Show Page Here), ⌘Z undo, a library panel and a companion window.
- Connected folders in sync with Finder (arrange by name, kind, date or tags).
- Touch ID lock, Report a Problem / Send Feedback, and a security pass (see `SECURITY.md`).
- Faster project switching and instant selection, even with 10,000 items.
