macOS menu bar app · October 2026

Stash

A quiet scrapbook for creative projects. Throw links, pictures, notes, files, music and video into it without leaving what you're doing — Stash keeps them in projects and folders, and as real files on your Mac.
The pop-up, hanging from the menu bar
The big window, everything in full

What it's for

  • Capture without switching. ⌥Space opens it over any app; paste, drop or press ⌥⌘V to save without opening it at all.
  • Organise like Finder. Projects as tabs, folders that sort themselves, free placement, colour tags, Get Info, Quick Look.
  • Nothing locked in. Every project and folder is a real folder on disk; drag anything out and it arrives as a real Mac file.

02 · Windows

The pop-up and the big window

Pop-up: tabs, the tool row, the board, the bottom bar
Inside a folder: path with Back, everything inside

The pop-up

  • Hangs under the menu bar icon; ⌥Space toggles it from any app.
  • Everything is a small icon tile with its name: pictures as thumbnails, files with their real Mac icons, links with their preview and a link badge.
  • Lock / Unlock (🔓 ▾): drag it anywhere by its top bar, lock it, and it reopens exactly there.

The big window

  • ⤢ expands; it can be moved and resized — or locked: draw a frame on screen (Draw Frame…) or Lock it where it is; it shows an orange 🔒 Locked.
  • Search (current tab only, folders and items), the ❝ minimal/full switch, Arrange By, Icons | List, the Organize wand.

The tool row

  • ↓ Project ▸ Folder always shows where new things will be saved.
  • 📋 Recent copies · 🔓 Lock · ❝ view · Arrange By ▾ · Icons | List · wand · expand. Each can be hidden in Settings → Toolbar.

The bottom bar

  • ⌘1 Note, ⌘2 Folder, ⌘3 Linker, ⌘4 PhotoBook, ⌘5 Notepad, ⌘6 Files — each with its own coloured icon.
  • 🗑 Clear empties the tab (two clicks, Undo, Recently Deleted), and ? lists every button.

05 · Music and video

MP3 and MP4, built in

A download that didn't work keeps a red ! with the reason and Try Again
  • MP3 / MP4 chips appear only on media links (YouTube and similar) and on video or sound files.
  • One click downloads (links, via the built-in downloader) or converts (files, via the Mac's own media tools and the built-in MP3 encoder) — nothing to install.
  • Progress on the thing itself: a see-through ring with the percent, and a small × to cancel.
  • The link moves inside the file: when the MP3/MP4 is ready it takes the link's place, and the link lives on as a small badge on its corner — open it, copy it, put it back on the board, or remove it. No extra copies on the board.
  • If it fails: a red ! stays on it — click for the reason, Try Again or Dismiss.
06 · Projects and moving around

Tabs for what you're working on

The project list (▾ next to the tabs)
  • Tabs show the pinned projects (📌) and the ones you opened last — as many as fit, always including the one you're in.
  • The ▾ list shows every project with how much is in it and a pin; click to open, right-click to rename, pin, save to Mac or delete.
  • + makes a new tab with its name box open.
  • Search looks only in the tab you're in, and finds folders and things by name anywhere inside it.

07 · Like Finder

Drag, Get Info, Quick Look

Dragging: the tile's picture follows the pointer; the real one waits, dimmed
Get Info (⌘I)
  • Dragging starts instantly, selected or not; over a folder it goes inside; out of the window it becomes a real Mac file (a copy) in Finder, the Desktop or any app. Dragging a project tab out gives the whole project as a folder.
  • Get Info shows kind, size, where it is, dates, the link, dimensions or duration, clickable colour tags, Show in Finder and a preview.
  • Quick Look (Space) is the real macOS one: pictures, video, music, PDFs.
  • ⌘C copies what's selected; several notes and links paste one after another.
08 · Safe and remembered

Undo, Recently Deleted, Recent copies

Recently Deleted, with the Undo message below
  • Undo in the message: deleting, moving, renaming, clearing, organising and finished downloads show a message with Undo.
  • Recently Deleted: everything deleted stays 30 days, even after a restart — Restore puts it back where it was.
  • Crash-safe saving: saves are checked before they replace the old file; the previous version and compressed hourly backups are kept, and a damaged library is restored automatically.
Recent copies (📋): the last 5 things you copied anywhere
  • Click one to copy it again, save it to the board, or drag it out. Files are only linked, so they take no space; secrets from password managers are never kept.

Settings: quick save, toolbar, storage
  • Quick save: choose the shortcut, keep copies of files, make typed folders when pasting.
  • Toolbar: turn each header tool on or off.
  • Storage: what Stash uses — your files, previews, Recently Deleted, library and backups, recent copies, temporary files, built-in tools — with safe clean-up buttons.
  • Save to Mac… (right-click a tab): a live copy of a project in any folder you choose, kept up to date as you change things.
  • Accessibility: VoiceOver names for every button, card and folder; Reduce Motion turns off animations.
Where your things live: ~/Documents/.Stash — one folder per project, one per folder, the real files inside (notes as .txt/.rtf, links as .webloc, pictures and media as themselves).
