## About (overview)

Swiss is a Mac app for people who collect things while they work.

It lives in the menu bar and opens with Option-Space. Drop, paste or drag in links, pictures, files and notes, and keep them in projects: one tab per project, with folders for each kind of thing. If you turn it on, Recent keeps what you copy and screenshot for a few days, and one tap keeps an item for good.

The magic wand sorts a messy board into folders by kind. Connected folders let you work in any Finder folder as if it were a project, and the changes happen in the real folder. The Frame is a canvas that floats above every app, for the references, notes and tutorials you want beside your work.

Everything stays on your Mac: no account, no servers, no analytics.

Swiss is made by Yotam Kiper. Feedback: kipery20@gmail.com
