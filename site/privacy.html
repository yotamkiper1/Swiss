Privacy

Short version: Swiss keeps your things on your Mac, and this website doesn't track you.

The Swiss app

  • No account and no Swiss servers. Nothing you save is sent to the developer.
  • Your library is stored as files on your Mac, in ~/Documents/.Stash, and settings in ~/Library/Application Support/Stash. If iCloud syncs your Documents folder, Apple syncs this folder as part of it.
  • Swiss goes online only for things you start: fetching a saved link's title and preview picture, a picture you drag in from a web page, a YouTube video you play on the Frame, a download you ask for, and updating its downloader tool. Those requests go to the sites involved.
  • Capture of the clipboard, screenshots and downloads is off until you turn it on. Items that apps mark as private (like passwords) are never kept, and password managers are excluded.
  • Report a Problem and Send Feedback open an email that you read and send yourself. Diagnostics are optional and hold versions, switches and counts, never library contents or file names.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no ads, no third-party fonts.
  • To show a download when one exists, your browser asks GitHub's public API (api.github.com) for Swiss's latest release. GitHub receives that request like any web request.
  • The hosting provider keeps standard server logs.
